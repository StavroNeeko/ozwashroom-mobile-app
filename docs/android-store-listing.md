# Google Play listing draft

For owner review after the standalone Android build is tested. These fields have not been submitted to Google Play.

**App name:** Ozwashroom

**Suggested category:** Shopping

**Short description:** Shop washroom supplies and check out securely with Ozwashroom

**Full description:**

Browse commercial washroom supplies with the Ozwashroom app.

Explore hand dryers, paper towel dispensers, soap dispensers, sanitary bins, baby change stations and grab rails. See product photos, prices in Australian dollars and availability from the Ozwashroom catalogue.

- Browse Special Deals, New Arrivals and discounted products.
- Search products and explore washroom categories.
- Save a cart on your device and adjust quantities.
- Complete your order through Shopify's secure browser checkout.
- Call Ozwashroom from the app for product enquiries.

An internet connection is required to browse the live catalogue and check out. Final prices, shipping, taxes and payment options are confirmed at checkout. Account sign-in and in-app order history are not currently available.
